Privacy Policy

Effective Date:

MOJA Labs (“we,” “our,” or “us”) develops mobile applications and software designed to be simple, privacy-friendly, and family-focused.

Most MOJA Labs applications do not require user accounts and do not collect personal information.

Information We Collect

Many MOJA Labs apps store information only locally on your device. Depending on the app, this may include:

  • App settings and preferences
  • Saved content and favorites
  • Notes, memories, or journal entries
  • Photos or videos you choose to attach

This information remains on your device unless you explicitly choose to export or share it.

Analytics and Third-Party Services

Some apps may use limited third-party services provided by platforms such as Apple to support:

  • App functionality
  • In-app purchases
  • Crash reporting
  • Basic analytics

These providers may collect limited technical information according to their own privacy policies.

MOJA Labs does not sell personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

Children’s Privacy

Our applications are designed to be family-friendly. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Storage

Unless explicitly stated otherwise within a specific application:

  • Data is stored locally on your device
  • No account is required
  • MOJA Labs does not provide cloud storage or synchronization services

In-App Purchases

Some applications may offer optional in-app purchases handled securely through Apple’s App Store.

MOJA Labs does not receive or store payment card information.

Your Choices

You may:

  • Delete app data by removing the app from your device
  • Remove saved content within supported apps
  • Manage app permissions through your device settings

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
